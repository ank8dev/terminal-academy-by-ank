// Brand guide

Terminal Academy brand

One look for the website and the command-line app: confident, friendly and a bit bold — a tech brand for teenagers, not a toy. Short mark: t//a. Tagline: Learn the terminal by doing.

// Logo

Logo variants

The primary mark is t//a on an Ink rounded square, with Yellow slashes and a Cyan cursor. All logo files are drawn as shapes, so they never depend on fonts.

Sizes

Use the small mark (slashes + cursor) at 32px and below, the primary mark from 48px up.

Clear space and rules

xx
  • Clear space on every side = x, the height of one slash.
  • Never stretch, squash, rotate or outline the logo.
  • Slashes are always Yellow — or Ink when the logo sits on a Yellow background.
  • Keep the Ink square; do not place the bare letters on busy photos.

// Colors

Color palette

The same palette is used on the web and in the terminal. ANSI 256 codes are the closest terminal colors.

  • Ink

    #1a1147 · ANSI 61 (dim)

    Main dark, text on light, icon background.

  • Ink-2

    #2a1d6b

    Gradients and dark surfaces.

  • Paper

    #fff8ec · ANSI 230

    Light background, text on dark.

  • Yellow

    #ffd23f · ANSI 221

    Brand accent, the //.

  • Pink

    #ff4f8b · ANSI 204

    Secondary accent, highlights, Ank.

  • Cyan

    #3ee0d0 · ANSI 80

    Cursor, links on dark.

  • Lime

    #9be564 · ANSI 149

    Success and checks.

Contrast: use Ink text on Paper, Yellow, Cyan, Lime and Pink. On Ink, use Paper, Yellow or Cyan text. Never put Pink text on Ink for body copy.

// Voice and tone

How we talk

Confident, friendly, clear. Short sentences. We treat learners as capable people doing something real.

Say

  • lesson
  • level 1–5
  • course
  • map of the course
  • practice
  • sandbox
  • practice folder
  • mentor (Ank)
  • progress
  • task
  • hint

Levels are real difficulty steps, not rewards.

Don't say

  • play
  • game
  • quest
  • level up
  • boss
  • badges
  • player
  • character
  • party
  • guild
  • XP
  • world
  • press start

No game metaphors, no emoji as decoration, no rotated stickers, no blinking calls to action.

Write
“Correct. grep -r searches a whole folder tree.”
Write
“The sandbox protects against accidents, not deliberate misuse.”
Avoid
Hype, exclamation chains, and promises we cannot keep (“100% safe”, “become a hacker”).
